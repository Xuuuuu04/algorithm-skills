There are three ways to choose a subset in sample case one. 1. Only participants with 3 points will get diplomas. 2. Participants with 2 or 3 points will get diplomas. 3. Everyone will get a diploma! The only option in sample case two is to award everyone. Note that in sample case three participants with zero scores cannot get anything.
